# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Its full text applies here; this page is a short summary.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their background, identity or level of experience.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and considerate, also when you disagree.
- Give and take feedback about the work, not about the person.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where it applies

In every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other channel the project uses, and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behaviour, tell the maintainers of CausalKernelLabs privately. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainers decide on a fair response, which can range from a warning to a ban from the project.
